• Leave the job title blank to match on your whole resume, or use a common title (e.g. "Software Engineer" rather than a company-specific one).
  • Lower or clear the minimum pay. Many postings don't publish a salary and get dropped by a high floor.
  • Widen the location. For remote, use a country like Remote USA; avoid narrow city-only locations, or just try Remote.
  • Set Work Type to "Any" if Remote / Hybrid / On-site is filtering too much.
  • Upload and select a resume. Matching leans on your Core Skills, Education, and Professional Development — a richer resume finds more.
  • List only the languages you need. Extra languages can exclude roles.
  • Re-run every couple of days. New jobs post constantly, so results change.
