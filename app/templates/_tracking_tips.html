• Add a job with ＋ Track. On any search result, click ＋ Track (or Apply first, then Track) to log it on your Applications page as Applied.
  • Keep each status current. Move a job along as things happen: Applied → Replied → Interview → Offer (or Rejected). Use Saved for roles you want to apply to later.
  • Watch your reply rate. It's replies ÷ applications sent — anything past Applied (Replied, Interview, Offer, or Rejected) counts as a reply. Saved jobs aren't counted as sent.
  • Log every application, even ones you found outside RoleQuill, so the reply-rate metric reflects your real pipeline.
  • Low reply rate? Tailor your resume to each role, apply sooner after a posting goes up, and favor roles where your Match / ATS scores are higher.
  • Follow up. No response in ~1–2 weeks? A short, polite nudge to the recruiter can move things along — then update the status here.
